## FU family
- **FU** = candle that (1) takes liquidity AND (2) breaks the previous candle high/low, then moves the opposite way within the same candle. Strong close / little rejection = higher quality. (guide ch.4; IMG_9329-9336)
- **ATT FU (A)** = manipulated but did NOT close as a strong FU; leaves small liquidity; often closes as a fake doji. **Strong FU (F)** = strong close. Marker legend: `A`=Attempted, `F`=Strong; bright=strong, faded=att; green=bull, red=bear.
- **Completion (R-120/121/122):** Complete FU = criteria met AND close inside previous candle body; ATT Form-1 = no new high/low; ATT Form-2 = new extreme but close NOT inside previous body.
- **FU retest quality (R-54):** 50% of the FU wick = strongest; wick touch = stronger; past 70% of the full FU with no wick touch = weak but still counts. Retest can be only 50% or 25% of the body. (IMG_9419)
- **x3 (R-213, final):** one candle containing BOTH FU and negation characteristics at macro level.
- **Negation:** opposite-direction FU on candle +1 or +2; must complete as a full FU — EXCEPT x3 (need not close as a full FU).

## HCS
- **HCS = a new FU retesting the last FU wick, inside an HTF orderblock.** HCS x2 / x3 stack. HCS is the best LTF entry / easiest setup. Gold HCS resolves on 1m, Forex HCS on 5m. (IMG_9416, IMG_9419, IMG_9341)
- **Eligible retest nodes: Strong FU, ATT FU, AND FU-negation — continuation OR negation form.** (IMPROVEMENT #2)
- **HTF re-test levels ladder (IMG_9416):** Lv1 = low of doji, Lv2 = 50% doji, Lv3 = high of doji, Lv4 = 50% of wick. If one level breaks, wait for the next.

## TFS + True Stop
- **TFS** = confirmed prevalent direction, evaluated only after candle close. Settings: macro / scalp / intraday / swing / extreme-swing (kept separate). ESTABLISHED vs AS-FORMING (as-forming never creates direction from zero). 10-min is the minimum to establish; sub-10m only refines.
- **True Stop / TSL (IMG_9425):** FU negation -> True Stop. HTF HCS = True Stop. TSL established at the START of the HCS: mark the high/low of the 2nd FU retesting the 1st FU; until the 1st FU high/low is broken WITH BODY, mark the TSL on the recent manipulation high/low. TS = the low/high where required 10m+ TFS factors align (Main POI); must be respected before an LTF entry.

## Liquidity
- **4 MAJOR (targetable) — IMG_9411:** unmanipulated doji (not AFU), perfect double tops/bottoms, perfect trendlines (3+ touches), imbalanced candles (5m+).
- **4 MINOR (ignore) — IMG_9413:** AFU, imperfect doubles, imperfect trendlines, balanced candles.
- **LAL/LAOL (IMG_9415):** the Last Area of Liquidity = the FIRST liquidity after an FU; most refined; reversal expected there if a major opposite target exists. Major liquidity != LAOL. LAOL sits inside a POI, near a True Stop.

## Entry models
